# SwiftSend – Project Description

## Summary

SwiftSend is a cross-border remittance and payment platform designed to simplify money transfers across African markets and between Africa and the global economy.

## What SwiftSend is

Built around Stellar blockchain infrastructure and integrated payment systems, SwiftSend aims to provide individuals, families, freelancers and businesses with a convenient, accessible and affordable way to send and receive money internationally.

The platform combines conventional financial infrastructure, blockchain-based settlement and local payment integrations to facilitate cross-border transfers. It also incorporates USSD technology to extend access to users who may not have smartphones or reliable internet connectivity.

## Current Development Stage

SwiftSend has a functional MVP, completed Stellar Anchor integration, a working USSD demonstration through Africa's Talking and simulated cross-border payment transactions. The platform is hosted at https://swiftsend.vendas.co.ke.

## Development Strategy

The project is progressing through five major stages:

1. MVP foundation and core payment functionality.
2. Payment infrastructure integration and customer validation.
3. Real payment provider integrations and transaction reliability.
4. Security, regulatory compliance and operational readiness.
5. Controlled pilot launch and expansion into additional markets.

## Initial Target Markets

Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Nigeria and the United States.

## Long-term Vision

To build an interoperable cross-border payment network that connects African financial systems with the global economy, making money movement simpler, faster, more affordable and accessible.
