## 9. Out-of-scope – explicitly not included

- Production implementation for corridors beyond the working countries listed above is not included in this Instaward.
- Full Production-Scale Operations – Long-term 24/7 operations, customer support, liquidity management, ongoing infrastructure management, and permanent operational staffing are outside the scope.
- Native Mobile Applications – Development and publication of dedicated Android or iOS application are not included. The MVP will focus on the supported web and USSD experiences.
- Advanced KYC/AML Infrastructure – Building a proprietary KYC/AML platform, compliance engine, or regulatory infrastructure is outside the scope. Where required, existing compliant infrastructure and partner processes will be used.
- Full Commercial Launch – A nationwide commercial launch, large-scale customer acquisition campaign, paid marketing campaign, or full public rollout is not included.
- Cryptocurrency Trading – SwiftSend will not provide cryptocurrency trading, speculative investment features, or an exchange marketplace. Stellar-based assets are used as settlement rails for remittance.
- Custom Banking Infrastructure – Building or replacing banking infrastructure, issuing banking products, or obtaining banking licences is outside the scope.
- Non-MVP Product Features – Features such as loyalty programs, lending, savings, advanced analytics, multi-currency wallets, or other non-essential product features will be deferred to future phases.
